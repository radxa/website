Product Details

The rCore-Q9075 is an edge computing module based on the Qualcomm IQ9075 processor. It delivers high computing power and exceptional energy efficiency, enabling stable handling of heavy workloads even in extreme environments while providing unparalleled on-device AI performance for demanding industrial applications.

Hardware Spec

  • SoC

    Qualcomm Dragonwing™ IQ-9075

  • CPU

    Octa-Core Kryo Gen 6 (Cortex-A78C based) @2.36 GHz

  • GPU

    Adreno 663 GPU

    - Delivers 1.2 TFLOPS FP32 with secure GPU compute

    - Supports Vulkan 1.2, OpenGL ES 3.2, OpenCL 2.0 FP

    - Supports Adreno NN Direct

  • MCU

    Quad-Core Cortex-R52 @ 1.85GHz

  • NPU

    200 TOPS@INT8 (Sparse Computing Power)

    - Features the Hexagon Tensor Processor (HTP) with quad HVX and dual HMX

    - Supports deep-learning frameworks: TensorFlow, PyTorch, ONNX, Paddle, Caffe, DarkNet, etc.

  • RAM

    36GB 96-bit LPDDR5@6400MT/s (with ECC support)

  • Audio DSP (LPASS)

    1980 MPPS, 7x TDM/I2S

    3x High-Speed I2S for Radio FE

  • Display Support

    Max 48 Mpix and 12 displays with no superframe

    5x 4K60, 3x 4K60 + 8x 1080p60, 2x DSI + 2x DP/eDP MST2 + 2x DP/eDP MST4

  • Video Decode

    Formats: AV1, H.264, H.265, VP9, MPEG2

    1x 8K60, 2x 8K30, 4x 4K60, 8x 4K30, 16x 1080p60, 32x 1080p30

  • Video Encode

    Formats: H.264, H.265, HEIF/ HEIC

    2x 4K60, 4x 4K30, 8x 1080p60, 16x 1080p30

  • Camera

    Up to 16 cameras, max 12 MP sensor resolution , 4x 4-lane CSI2

  • PCIe

    2x PCIe Gen 4: 1x 2-lane + 1x 4-lane (Root Complex & Endpoint)

  • USB

    2x USB 3.1 Gen 2, 1x USB 2.0

  • Networking

    2x 2.5 GbE w/ TSN (SGMII)

  • Other I/O

    21x QUP_SEs (supports UART/I2C/SPI), 149x GPIOs

  • Storage

    2x UFS 3.1 Gen 4 2-lane, 1x 8-bit SDCC5, NVMe over PCIe

  • WiFi/BT/WAN

    Support through companion chips: QCA6698AQ , SDX35 , SDX72

  • Operating Conditions

    -40°C to 115°C (Tj)

  • Software

    Linux Yocto, Linux Ubuntu

  • Dimension

    65 mm × 65 mm
