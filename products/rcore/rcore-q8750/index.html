Product Details

Radxa rCore-Q8750 is a flagship edge AI system-on-module powered by Qualcomm Dragonwing Q-8750, featuring a 3nm 8-core Oryon CPU up to 4.32 GHz, up to 77 Dense TOPS of Hexagon AI performance, and an Adreno 830 GPU. It supports up to 24 GB of 64-bit LPDDR5X, on-module 128GB UFS, up to 8K60 video decode and 8K30 video encode, up to two 4-lane MIPI DSI interfaces, and up to six 4-lane MIPI CSI interfaces. PCIe Gen 3 x2, USB 3.1 Gen 2, and QUP interfaces enable expansion in a 36 × 32 mm, 516-ball BGA package, with longevity through 2036.

    Compact Module. Flagship Edge AI and Vision.

    Powered by the Qualcomm Dragonwing™ Q-8750 processor platform, rCore-Q8750 brings high-performance computing, advanced multimedia, multi-camera awareness, and optional on-device AI acceleration into a compact 36 × 32 mm form factor.

    8K Video. Multi-Display Freedom

    An integrated Adreno 8-series multimedia pipeline enables high-resolution video decode, encode, and display output for smart displays, media stations, collaboration systems, and edge video devices.

    • Video Decode: H.265/H.264/VP9/AV1, up to 4K240 or 8K60
    • Video Encode: H.264/H.265, up to 4K120 or 8K30
    • Concurrent 4K60 decode and 4K60 encode

    • Up to 2× 4-lane MIPI DSI
    • DisplayPort 1.4 over USB-C, up to 8K60 with MST

    Advanced Multi-Camera Vision

    Six 4-lane MIPI CSI interfaces and the Qualcomm Spectra ISP enable high-resolution, multi-camera perception for robotics, intelligent imaging, and machine vision.

    Qualcomm Spectra ISP 8-series

    • Up to 6× 4-lane MIPI CSI
    • Up to 5 cameras operating concurrently
    • Up to 3× 48 MP @ 30 fps ZSL
    • Up to 320 MP non-ZSL snapshot
