Product Details

Radxa rCore-Q8550 is a flagship edge AI system-on-module for robotics, machine vision, and intelligent edge devices, powered by the Qualcomm® Dragonwing™ QCS8550 platform. Its 4nm 8-core Qualcomm® Kryo™ CPU, Adreno™ 740 GPU, and Hexagon™ AI Processor work together to handle high-performance computing, visual processing, and on-device AI inference, with an optional NPU configuration available to suit project requirements. The module supports up to 16GB of 64-bit LPDDR5X, 128GB UFS 3.1 and 32 MB SPI NOR on-module storage, up to 8K60 video decode, 8K30 video encode, dual 4K60 displays, and up to six 4-lane MIPI CSI interfaces. PCIe Gen 4, PCIe Gen 3, USB 3.1 Gen 2, and versatile QUP interfaces provide flexible expansion in a compact 36 x 32 mm BGA package.
